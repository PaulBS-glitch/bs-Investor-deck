BuySooner
Institutional Investor Presentation

Residential contribution-layer finance for deposit-constrained, mortgage-capable Australian home buyers.

BuySooner was formed to solve a structural deposit-gap problem for mortgage-capable Australian home buyers. The platform contributes a defined settlement layer alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage, helping eligible customers buy earlier while creating controlled contribution-layer exposure for institutional capital.

01

Market dislocation

Serviceable buyers are constrained by deposit formation, rent leakage and the time required to accumulate sufficient equity.

02

Capital solution

BuySooner contributes a defined settlement layer alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage, enabling eligible buyers to transact earlier.

03

Investor exposure

Capital is deployed into governed contribution positions with eligibility controls, concentration limits, exit testing and fallback pathways.
