BuySooner
Institutional Investor Presentation

Presentation focus

Residential contribution-layer finance for deposit-constrained, mortgage-capable Australian home buyers.

Opening proposition

BuySooner addresses a structural deposit-gap problem by providing a defined contribution layer alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage.

For institutional capital, the opportunity is a governed contribution-layer portfolio supported by credit policy, portfolio controls, refinance discipline and defined exit pathways.

01 / Investment context

Mortgage-capable demand is delayed.

Buyers may be constrained by deposit formation rather than serviceability.

02 / Product position

BuySooner funds the missing layer.

The platform contributes a defined settlement layer beside customer equity and a senior mortgage.

03 / Investor frame

The exposure is governed and controlled.

Credit policy, refinance discipline, portfolio controls and downside controls define the investment frame.
