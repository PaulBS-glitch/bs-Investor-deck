BuySooner
Institutional Investor Presentation

Residential contribution-layer finance for deposit-constrained, mortgage-capable Australian home buyers.

Opening proposition

BuySooner addresses a structural deposit-gap problem for mortgage-capable Australian home buyers.

The platform provides a defined contribution layer alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage, helping eligible customers buy earlier.

For investors, the opportunity is governed contribution-layer exposure supported by policy, portfolio controls and defined exit pathways.

Investment logic
01

Investment context

Mortgage-capable buyers are delayed by deposit formation, not necessarily by serviceability.

02

Product position

BuySooner contributes a defined settlement layer beside customer equity and a senior mortgage.

03

Investor frame

The opportunity is governed contribution-layer exposure, supported by credit policy, refinance discipline and downside controls.
