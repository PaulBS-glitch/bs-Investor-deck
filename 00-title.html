Opening proposition

BuySooner addresses a structural deposit-gap problem for mortgage-capable Australian home buyers.

The platform provides a defined contribution layer alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage, helping eligible customers buy earlier while creating governed contribution-layer exposure for institutional capital.
