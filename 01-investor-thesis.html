02 / The problem

The deposit target keeps moving while buyers save.

Problem BuySooner solves

Many buyers can support a mainstream senior mortgage, but cannot accumulate the deposit, transaction costs and settlement capital quickly enough.

Core problem

A buyer may be mortgage-capable but still unable to transact. The issue is not necessarily income, credit quality or serviceability.

The issue is timing: the buyer cannot complete the upfront capital stack before rent, price growth and transaction costs move the goalposts again.
