02 / The problem

Mortgage-capable buyers are locked out by the deposit gap.

The problem is not always affordability. In many cases, the buyer can support a mainstream senior mortgage but cannot accumulate the deposit and transaction capital quickly enough.

A large segment of Australian buyers remain outside ownership while they continue paying rent, remain exposed to property-price movement and risk losing access to suitable properties. BuySooner targets this timing gap: serviceable borrowers delayed by deposit formation, not weak-credit customers requiring affordability repair.

01

Price movement while saving

Buyers saving for a deposit remain exposed to property-price movement before they can enter the market.

02

Rent leakage

Rent reduces the borrower’s ability to build deposit equity while they remain outside ownership.

03

Deposit formation delay

For otherwise mortgage-capable buyers, the deposit timeline becomes the binding constraint.

Institutional takeaway: the target segment is mortgage-capable but deposit-constrained, creating a contribution-layer opportunity rather than a weak-credit lending proposition.
