02 / The problem

Mortgage-capable buyers are locked out by a moving deposit target.

The issue is not always affordability. Many buyers can support a mainstream senior mortgage, but cannot accumulate the deposit, transaction costs and settlement capital quickly enough.

Core problem

A large segment of Australian buyers remains outside ownership despite being capable of servicing a senior mortgage. The binding constraint is often upfront capital, not weak credit.

The problem compounds because the target can keep moving while the buyer saves: rent continues, property prices in major capital cities may rise faster than deposit formation, transaction costs remain payable and suitable property choice can narrow.
