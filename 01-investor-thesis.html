01 / Investor thesis

BuySooner bridges the deposit gap for mortgage-capable buyers.

BuySooner contributes a defined capital layer at purchase so customers can transact earlier, while the customer remains the legal owner and the senior mortgage is provided by a bank or non-bank lender.

Customer problem

Customers can often service a mortgage but cannot accumulate the final deposit fast enough.

BuySooner role

Provides a structured contribution layer, typically around the deposit gap, with repayment targeted at refinance or sale.

Investor proposition

Exposure to a controlled contribution layer, supported by borrower equity, senior lender underwriting and exit governance.

Deck logic: problem → solution → customer example → BuySooner economics → investor capital model → credit controls → downside → facility ask.
