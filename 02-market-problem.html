03 / Market failure

The existing pathways do not solve the missing capital layer.

What this slide proves

The market problem is not a lack of buyer demand. It is that the usual options either delay the transaction, add leverage, rely on family wealth or force a poorer asset choice.

Market gap

Brokers already see customers who can support a mainstream home loan but cannot bridge the deposit gap quickly enough. Existing pathways do not convert that demand into a scalable, governed transaction.

Pathway

What it does

Why it fails

01

Waiting

The buyer keeps renting and saving while the property target may keep moving.

Delays ownership rather than solving the capital stack.

02

High-LVR / LMI

The buyer may transact using more senior debt, higher cost and tighter lender appetite.

Adds leverage. It does not create a separate contribution layer.

03

Family support

Parental assistance may bridge the gap for some buyers.

Access depends on household wealth, not borrower quality.

04

Buying down

The buyer lowers the target purchase price or compromises on location or property type.

Solves the deposit maths by weakening asset quality and customer fit.

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner fills the structural gap these pathways leave open: a governed contribution layer that helps eligible buyers transact while preserving senior-lender underwriting.
