The Solution: BuySooner Bridges the Deposit Gap

BuySooner contributes a defined capital layer at settlement, alongside customer equity and a senior mortgage, allowing eligible customers to buy earlier while preserving a conventional mortgage structure.

01

Customer benefit

Eligible customers can buy earlier, reduce the period spent renting and saving, and enter ownership while remaining the legal owner of the property.

02

Senior lender alignment

The senior mortgage remains central. A bank or non-bank lender still performs its own credit assessment and holds the first mortgage.

03

Investor benefit

Capital is deployed into a defined contribution layer, governed by eligibility, contribution limits, exit testing and fallback controls.

Illustrative capital stack
Senior Mortgage80%
Customer Equity10%
BuySooner Contribution10%

BuySooner funds the defined contribution layer, not the whole property acquisition.

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner does not replace the senior lender. It funds a controlled contribution layer within a governed residential capital stack.
