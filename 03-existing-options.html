04 / The solution

BuySooner fills the missing capital layer at settlement.

What BuySooner does

BuySooner contributes a defined settlement layer alongside customer equity and the senior mortgage, helping eligible customers transact earlier without replacing bank underwriting.

Illustrative capital stack
Senior mortgage
80%
Customer equity
10%
BuySooner contribution
10%

BuySooner funds only the incremental settlement gap, not the whole property.

01 / Customer outcome

Earlier ownership.

Eligible customers can move from saving and renting into ownership earlier, subject to senior lender and BuySooner approval.

02 / Senior lender alignment

The bank remains central.

The senior lender keeps its ordinary credit role and holds the first mortgage.

03 / Investor exposure

Defined contribution exposure.

Investor capital is deployed into a controlled contribution layer, governed by eligibility rules, contribution caps, exit testing and fallback pathways.

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner does not replace the senior lender. It provides a controlled settlement contribution within a governed residential capital stack.
