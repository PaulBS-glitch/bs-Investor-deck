05 / How BuySooner works

BuySooner contributes at settlement and exits at refinance or sale.

Operating model

The customer owns the property from day one, pays the senior mortgage, and has no monthly BuySooner repayment during the term.

01

Eligibility and application

Customer is assessed against BuySooner eligibility, contribution limits and exit viability.

02

Senior mortgage approval

The customer obtains a senior mortgage from a bank or non-bank lender. The senior lender remains first ranking.

03

BuySooner contribution

BuySooner contributes the agreed capital layer at settlement to complete the purchase capital stack.

04

Customer ownership

The customer is the legal owner from day one and occupies or holds the property in the ordinary way.

05

During the term

The customer pays the senior mortgage. BuySooner does not charge monthly repayments, interest or compounding during the term.

06

Exit

BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale, with exit governed by the agreed formula, review process and credit policy.

Simple operating model

Assess, contribute, monitor, exit.

BuySooner assesses eligibility, contributes at settlement, monitors the position and manages exit through refinance or sale.

Customer payment position

No monthly BuySooner repayment.

No monthly BuySooner repayment, no interest-style monthly charge and no compounding during the term.

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner is a structured contribution-layer model. It is not a replacement mortgage, not a monthly repayment product and not a shared-ownership model.
