06 / Prototype example

The prototype demonstrates the customer benefit in a transaction scenario.

Question this page answers

What changes for the customer if the missing capital layer is supplied at settlement instead of waiting to save the final deposit amount?

Scenario purpose

This page links the investor deck to the interactive customer roadmap. It shows that BuySooner is not an abstract funding concept: it changes the ownership timeline, the cashflow path and the customer’s exposure to a moving market.

Waiting pathway

Ownership

Customer remains outside ownership while saving.

Capital gap

Deposit and transaction costs still need to be accumulated.

Market risk

Customer remains exposed to price movement and property choice narrowing.

Cashflow

Rent continues while the customer is still trying to save.

BuySooner pathway

Ownership

Customer enters ownership earlier, subject to senior lender and BuySooner assessment.

Capital gap

The missing contribution layer is supplied at settlement.

Market risk

Customer moves from saving outside the market to owning the asset.

Cashflow

Customer pays the senior mortgage. No monthly BuySooner repayment during the term.

Illustrative purchase

$1,000,000 property

Used to demonstrate the customer-facing roadmap and the investment capital stack.

Structure

80 / 10 / 10

Senior mortgage $800,000, customer equity $100,000 and BuySooner contribution $100,000.

Operating result

Own earlier, exit later.

Customer owns from day one. BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale under the agreed exit framework.

Institutional takeaway: the prototype converts the product thesis into a visible customer journey: BuySooner fills the missing contribution layer, moves the customer into ownership earlier, and preserves the senior mortgage structure.
