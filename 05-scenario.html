06 / Prototype example

Buying sooner changes the customer’s ownership timeline.

The BuySooner contribution helps an eligible customer complete the purchase capital stack earlier, rather than remaining outside the market while saving the final deposit amount.

Waiting pathway

Customer continues renting while saving the final deposit amount.

Ownership timingDelayed
Deposit savingContinues
Market exposureStill exposed to price movement
CashflowRent continues
Purchase certaintyUncertain timing

BuySooner pathway

Customer buys earlier using a defined BuySooner contribution layer.

Ownership timingEarlier
Deposit gapFilled by defined contribution
Market exposureCustomer enters ownership sooner
CashflowCustomer pays senior mortgage
Purchase certaintySubject to credit and exit tests

Illustrative purchase

$1,000,000 property purchase used to show the capital stack.

Structure

Senior mortgage $800,000, customer equity $100,000, BuySooner contribution $100,000.

Operating outcome

Customer owns the property from day one, pays the senior mortgage only, and BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale.

Institutional takeaway: the worked example demonstrates the core thesis: BuySooner brings forward the purchase date for eligible customers while creating a defined, governed contribution-layer exposure for capital providers.
