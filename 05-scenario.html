06 / Transaction example

BuySooner supplies the missing settlement contribution.

Question this page answers

What changes if the customer can complete the capital stack at settlement instead of continuing to rent and save?

Scenario purpose

This page shows the practical difference between waiting and using a controlled BuySooner contribution at settlement.

Waiting pathway

Ownership

Customer remains outside ownership while saving.

Capital gap

Deposit and transaction costs still need to be accumulated.

Market exposure

Customer remains exposed to price movement and narrowing property choice.

Cashflow

Rent continues while the customer is still trying to save.

BuySooner pathway

Ownership

Customer enters ownership earlier, subject to senior lender and BuySooner approval.

Capital gap

BuySooner supplies the agreed contribution layer at settlement.

Market exposure

Customer moves from saving outside the market to owning the asset.

Cashflow

Customer pays the senior mortgage only. No monthly BuySooner payment during the term.

Illustrative purchase

$1,000,000 property

Used to demonstrate the transaction structure and customer pathway.

Structure

80 / 10 / 10

Senior mortgage $800,000, customer equity $100,000 and BuySooner contribution $100,000.

Operating result

Own earlier, repay at exit.

Customer owns from day one. BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale under the agreed exit framework.

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner converts a delayed transaction into a structured ownership pathway by supplying the missing settlement contribution while preserving the senior mortgage structure.
