06 / BuySooner economics

BuySooner is repaid at exit.

The customer has no monthly BuySooner repayment during the term. BuySooner receives its agreed payment at refinance or sale.

ItemDescription
Initial contributionCapital provided at settlement.
Exit participationAgreed participation calculated under the product formula.
Exit eventRefinance or sale, subject to credit and legal process.
