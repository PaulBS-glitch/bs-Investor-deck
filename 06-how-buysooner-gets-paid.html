07 / The business model

Revenue is realised when the contribution-layer position exits.

Commercial model

BuySooner contributes capital at settlement and realises its return when the customer refinances or sells, using a pre-agreed exit formula rather than monthly interest or compounding.

01

Contribution at settlement

BuySooner deploys a defined settlement contribution into an eligible transaction to complete the upfront capital stack.

02

No monthly BuySooner payment

The customer pays the senior mortgage during the term. BuySooner charges no monthly payment, no interest and no compounding.

03

Exit realisation

BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale. The exit amount equals the initial contribution plus the formula-based participation amount.

Commercial drivers

Contribution size

Property value at exit

Time outstanding

Agreed pricing multiplier

Transaction volume

Portfolio performance

Institutional takeaway: BuySooner is not a monthly interest model. It is a transaction-and-exit model: capital is deployed into defined contribution-layer positions and value is realised when customers refinance or sell.
