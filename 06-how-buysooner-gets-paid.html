07 / The business model

BuySooner earns at exit, not through monthly customer repayments.

BuySooner contributes capital at settlement and receives its agreed repayment at refinance or sale under the product formula.

01

Initial contribution

BuySooner provides a defined contribution layer at settlement to help complete the customer’s purchase capital stack.

02

No monthly BuySooner repayments

The customer pays the senior mortgage. BuySooner does not charge monthly repayments, interest or compounding during the term.

03

Exit payment

BuySooner is repaid at refinance or sale, with the exit amount governed by the agreed formula and credit policy.

Revenue drivers

Revenue is driven by contribution size, property value at exit, exit timing, formula settings and transaction volume.

Commercial logic

The model converts delayed, serviceable demand into completed transactions and creates value when the customer refinances or sells.

Institutional takeaway: the business model is transaction and exit-led: capital is deployed into defined contribution positions, with value realised when customers refinance or sell.
