07 / Investor capital model

Investor capital funds eligible contribution positions.

The facility funds BuySooner contribution positions that sit behind the senior mortgage and are governed by underwriting, monitoring, exit review and recovery controls.

QuestionAnswer
What is funded?Eligible BuySooner contribution positions.
How is capital repaid?Refinance or sale proceeds at exit.
What controls the exposure?Contribution limits, property eligibility, senior lender underwriting and exit viability testing.
