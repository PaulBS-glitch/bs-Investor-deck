08 / Institutional facility model

Investor capital funds diversified contribution-layer positions, not whole-property exposure.

The proposed AUD $100 million warehouse facility is structured around capital recovery, liquidity serviceability and lender protection, with exposure limited to the BuySooner contribution layer.

Facility

AUD $100 million

Senior secured warehouse facility designed to fund BuySooner contribution positions.

Diversification

Approximately 800 positions

Average exposure of approximately $125,000 per contribution position.

Exposure

Narrow contribution layer

The facility funds the BuySooner layer, not the customer’s whole property purchase.

Illustrative capital stack

Senior mortgage80%
Borrower equity10%
BuySooner contribution10%

Eight-layer protection framework

Borrower equity, senior lender underwriting, BuySooner underwriting, eligibility controls, mandatory P&I amortisation, Stage 2 governance, recovery waterfall and portfolio refinanceability.

Institutional takeaway: the lender is underwriting capital recovery and liquidity serviceability, not a speculative housing-growth thesis.
