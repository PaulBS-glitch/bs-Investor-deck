08 / Capital model

Investor capital funds the BuySooner contribution layer, not the whole property.

Investable position

BuySooner aggregates defined contribution positions originated through eligible home-buyer transactions. Each position is linked to a customer-owned residential property and is expected to exit through refinance or sale.

Transaction capital stack

Senior mortgage

First-ranking lender exposure underwritten and held by the senior lender.

Customer equity

Borrower capital is committed from day one and provides alignment and a first-loss buffer before the BuySooner contribution is impaired.

BuySooner contribution

Defined contribution-layer position funded by investor capital and governed by eligibility, contribution limits, exit testing and recovery controls.

01 / Diversified positions

Exposure is pooled across transactions.

Capital is deployed across multiple customer transactions rather than into a single asset or borrower.

02 / Narrow exposure layer

The investor funds the contribution position.

Exposure is limited to the BuySooner contribution layer, although exit value is linked to the property value at refinance or sale.

03 / Exit-led recovery

Repayment is expected through refinance or sale.

Outcomes are governed by credit policy, exit testing, portfolio monitoring and fallback controls.

Institutional takeaway: The investor is not funding whole-property acquisition exposure. The investable position is a diversified, governed contribution-layer portfolio with defined recovery and liquidity controls.
