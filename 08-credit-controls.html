09 / Risk management

Capital protection comes from layered controls, not a single assumption.

Investor question

How is contribution-layer capital protected before the model scales into a larger portfolio?

01

Borrower and senior lender screen

The customer must support the senior mortgage. The senior lender performs its own assessment and remains first ranking.

02

BuySooner credit controls

BuySooner assesses eligibility, contribution size, property suitability, exit viability and policy compliance before funding.

03

Portfolio controls

Exposure is managed by geography, property type, borrower profile, contribution band, vintage and exit timing.

04

Exit discipline

The model is designed around refinance or sale, with monitoring and fallback controls supporting capital recovery.

Borrower equity

Capital at risk from day one.

The customer contributes equity into the transaction before BuySooner is repaid at exit.

Underwriting

Dual assessment.

The senior lender tests the mortgage while BuySooner tests contribution exposure and exit viability.

Portfolio monitoring

Ongoing control.

Concentrations, conduct, valuations, contribution bands and exit timing can be monitored over time.

Fallback pathway

Refinance or sale.

Exit is governed through refinance or sale rather than indefinite exposure.

Institutional takeaway: the investor is not relying only on property growth. Protection comes from layered underwriting, borrower equity, controlled contribution sizing, portfolio monitoring and defined exit pathways.
