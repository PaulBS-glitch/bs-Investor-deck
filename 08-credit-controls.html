09 / Credit controls and lender protection

The facility is protected by layered underwriting, monitoring and recovery controls.

BuySooner converts the contribution-layer thesis into lender controls: eligibility rules, borrower testing, portfolio limits, reserve mechanics, Stage 2 governance and sale fallback.

01

Borrower and senior-lender controls

Verified income, DTI cap, residual income assessment, acceptable mortgage conduct and mandatory principal-and-interest senior mortgage.

02

Property and portfolio controls

Approved collateral, liquidity screens, contribution limits and concentration limits by borrower, postcode, region, state, property type, vintage and valuation provider.

03

Exit controls

Every exposure must have a credible refinance or sale pathway and be classified as Full Exit Viable, Partial Exit Viable or Exit Not Viable.

04

Liquidity and recovery controls

Six-month interest reserve, Stage 2 utilisation monitoring, cash sweep triggers, recovery waterfall and sale fallback.

Eligibility gate

Ineligible assets are excluded or haircut from the borrowing base, preserving lender protection as the portfolio changes.

Dynamic monitoring

Monthly and quarterly reporting tracks utilisation, Stage 2 migration, concentration metrics, arrears, valuation refreshes and covenant compliance.

Failure sequence

Principal impairment requires multiple layers to fail, including borrower equity, amortisation, liquidity management, refinanceability and recovery.

Institutional takeaway: the protection case is not one control. It is the redundancy of multiple controls operating before lender principal is impaired.
