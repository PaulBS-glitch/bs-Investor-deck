08 / Credit controls

The product is controlled by eligibility and exit viability.

The investor story needs to show that BuySooner is not relying only on growth. It is governed by upfront eligibility, senior lender underwriting, contribution bands and formal exit review.

ControlPurpose
Customer contributionBorrower has equity from day one.
Senior lender assessmentIndependent serviceability and mortgage underwriting.
Contribution bandsControls exposure size and exception handling.
Year 3 reviewTests refinance, partial exit or fallback pathway.
