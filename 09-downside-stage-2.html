10 / Market opportunity

The opportunity is a defined segment, not broad property ownership.

Investor question

Is there a repeatable market segment where a contribution-layer product can convert delayed, mortgage-capable demand into completed transactions?

01

Defined customer segment

BuySooner targets mortgage-capable buyers who are constrained by deposit formation, transaction costs and settlement capital, not by weak credit.

02

Visible origination problem

The deposit gap appears directly in broker and property conversations, making the problem observable before the transaction is delayed or lost.

03

Contribution-layer wedge

BuySooner does not need to fund whole properties or replace the senior mortgage. It funds the missing contribution layer for eligible transactions.

Demand source

Mortgage-capable but deposit-constrained.

The addressable segment is customers with borrowing capacity who cannot assemble the final settlement capital quickly enough.

Channel fit

Broker-visible and referral-led.

The problem surfaces naturally at the point where customers seek finance, property access or transaction support.

Portfolio potential

Repeatable contribution positions.

The opportunity is to originate many defined contribution-layer exposures across eligible customers, assets, geographies and vintages.

Institutional takeaway: the market opportunity is repeatable contribution-layer finance for a defined segment of mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained buyers, not broad property ownership.
