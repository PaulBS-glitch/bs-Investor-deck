09 / Downside and Stage 2

Downside is handled through defined pathways.

The deck should make clear what happens if refinance is delayed or market assumptions are weaker than expected.

PathwayMeaning
Full exit viableCustomer can refinance and repay BuySooner in full.
Partial exit viablePartial refinance or other controlled pathway is available.
Exit not viableEscalation, fallback management and recovery process applies.
Stage 2Controlled fallback process, not an automatic loss event.
