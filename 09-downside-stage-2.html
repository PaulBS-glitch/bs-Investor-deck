10 / Market opportunity

A large addressable market exists inside the deposit-constrained buyer segment.

BuySooner targets customers who can support a senior mortgage but are delayed by deposit formation, transaction costs and the time required to assemble the final capital layer.

Demand

Mortgage-capable buyers

The target customer is not weak-credit. The customer has borrowing capacity but lacks the full settlement capital required to transact now.

Timing

Deposit gap friction

While customers save, they remain exposed to rent leakage, property price movement and loss of suitable property access.

Channel

Broker-visible problem

The deposit gap appears directly in mortgage conversations, creating a natural origination channel through brokers and referral partners.

Product wedge

BuySooner does not need to replace the senior mortgage. It only needs to fund the defined contribution layer for eligible transactions.

Portfolio opportunity

A $100 million facility can fund approximately 800 diversified contribution positions at an average exposure of around $125,000.

Institutional takeaway: the opportunity is not broad property ownership. It is repeatable contribution-layer finance for a defined segment of mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained buyers.
