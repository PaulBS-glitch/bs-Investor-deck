11 / Readiness and execution plan

BuySooner has moved from concept into controlled execution planning.

Investor question

Is BuySooner still an idea, or is it being converted into an executable pilot and lender diligence pathway?

01

Product architecture defined

Core mechanics, contribution structure, customer ownership position, exit formula and review process are documented.

02

Credit and controls translated

Eligibility, contribution bands, exit viability, monitoring, exception handling and recovery pathways are being converted into operating controls.

03

Prototype pathway built

The roadmap prototype demonstrates how the customer journey, scenario logic, education pathway and report generation can work.

04

Pilot operating layer

Broker workflow, application intake, file assessment, data capture and reporting can be developed into a controlled pilot process.

05

Lender diligence pathway

Facility materials, portfolio reporting, model assumptions, data-room requirements and term-sheet inputs are being prepared for institutional review.

Pilot objectives

Validate demand

Test eligibility

Prove workflow

Confirm controls

Generate data

Prepare reporting

Institutional takeaway: the next phase is not product invention. It is execution proof: validating demand, process, controls, data capture and lender reporting through a controlled pilot pathway.
