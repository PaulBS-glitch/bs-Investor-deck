12 / Investment ask

Funding converts BuySooner into a controlled contribution-layer portfolio.

The investment ask is to support staged deployment into eligible BuySooner contribution positions, validate the operating model and prepare the platform for institutional facility scale.

Use of funds

Eligible contribution positions

Deploy capital into approved BuySooner contribution layers for mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained customers.

Build

Technology and operating capability

Support scenario tooling, broker workflow, application intake, monitoring, reporting and data-room readiness.

Prototype

Customer roadmap demonstration

Use the prototype roadmap to show how the customer journey, scenario output and education layer can be presented in practice.

Scale

Institutional facility pathway

Build the portfolio data, controls and reporting required for larger warehouse or institutional funding discussions.

Immediate next step

Agree the funding pathway, diligence requirements, pilot parameters and investor reporting cadence.

What funding delivers

A controlled pilot portfolio, operational proof, lender-ready data and the evidence base for larger institutional capital.

Core investor proposition

Defined contribution-layer exposure to a repeatable housing finance problem, governed through credit, portfolio and exit controls.

Closing proposition: BuySooner turns the deposit-gap problem into a structured, fundable and repeatable contribution-layer finance model, with the prototype roadmap showing how the customer experience can be demonstrated.
