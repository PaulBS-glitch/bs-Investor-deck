BuySooner Appendix

Customer-facing broker/customer experience calculator.

Purpose

Demonstrate the customer and broker pathway.

This appendix shows how a broker or customer could move from basic scenario inputs into a personalised BuySooner roadmap, with the calculator acting as the bridge between explanation, education and customer-specific output.

01

Data capture

Captures customer status, target price, location, deposit position and current housing cost.

02

Confirmation

Shows the customer what has been captured before generating the personalised pathway.

03

Personalised roadmap

Turns the scenario into a customer-facing BuySooner journey and report-style output.

Appendix takeaway: the calculator demonstrates how the BuySooner proposition can be explained, personalised and moved into a customer-facing roadmap.
