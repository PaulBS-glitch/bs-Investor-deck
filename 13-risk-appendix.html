Appendix 3 / How BuySooner evaluates risk

How BuySooner evaluates risk.

BuySooner uses layered underwriting: the bank assesses the first mortgage, then BuySooner assesses the contribution layer, property, portfolio fit and exit pathway.

01

Senior lender underwriting

The bank or non-bank lender assesses the first mortgage, including serviceability, credit conduct, borrower capacity and first-ranking security.

02

BuySooner underwriting

BuySooner separately assesses contribution size, collateral quality, borrower profile, exit pathway and portfolio concentration before approving the contribution layer.

03

Exit viability gate

A transaction is not approved unless there is a credible refinance or sale exit under conservative assumptions within the policy period.

04

Review and exit discipline

Year 3 review, updated valuation, refinance assessment and exit classification drive the pathway to full refinance, partial exit, sale or escalation.

Risk controls

Risk is managed through process, not price-growth hope.

Bank serviceability test

Customer equity from day one

Controlled contribution limits

Property and concentration controls

Exit discipline

Refinance first. Sale fallback.

The preferred exit is refinance. If that is not viable, BuySooner uses partial exit, sale or recovery processes to protect capital.

Investor takeaway: BuySooner does not rely on a single approval point. Risk is evaluated through senior lender underwriting, BuySooner credit assessment, exit viability testing, scheduled review and refinance-first execution discipline.
