Appendix 8 / Live proof asset

Broker-led customer experience tool.

Demonstrates how a broker explains BuySooner, models the customer pathway and supports the conversation before assessment.

Purpose

Demonstrate the broker-led customer conversation.

The calculator shows how a broker can take a customer from an initial deposit-gap problem through the benefits of BuySooner, an indicative purchase structure, plain-English FAQs and the pathway toward assessment.

01

Customer scenario

Captures the customer’s purchase target, current position, deposit gap and housing-cost context.

02

Benefit explanation

Shows how BuySooner may help the customer buy sooner, reduce waiting time and move from delay into a structured purchase pathway.

03

Indicative modelling

Models the customer contribution, senior loan, BuySooner contribution and likely refinance or sale pathway.

04

FAQs and next steps

Supports the broker with plain-English explanations, limitations, customer questions and the next step toward formal assessment.

Appendix takeaway: the calculator is a broker-led explanation and modelling tool, helping turn the BuySooner proposition into a practical customer conversation before formal submission.
