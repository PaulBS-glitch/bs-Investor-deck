Appendix 5 / Illustrative economics and investor return profile

Single-transaction economics explain the return pathway.

The example starts with a $1 million transaction so the unit economics are clear before moving to portfolio, facility and investor waterfall terms.

Investor interpretation

This shows transaction-level economics only. It is not a promised investor return. Actual investor outcome depends on facility pricing, funding costs, portfolio performance, losses, Stage 2 utilisation and final waterfall terms.

Appendix takeaway: the $1 million example explains the unit economics. The investor case is created by repeating controlled contribution-layer positions across a diversified portfolio, with disciplined origination, liquidity coverage, recovery controls and institutional reporting.
