Appendix 1 / Customer eligibility and exclusions

Define who BuySooner is for, and who it is not for.

This appendix frames the eligible customer, property and contribution settings that keep the model focused on mortgage-capable buyers, not weak-credit or distressed borrowers.

01

Target customer

Mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained buyers who can support the senior mortgage but are delayed by upfront settlement capital.

02

Customer equity required

The customer contributes capital from day one, creating alignment and a loss buffer before the BuySooner contribution is impaired.

03

Excluded cases

Weak-credit, distressed, speculative or non-standard customer/property scenarios should remain outside the core pilot pathway.

Purpose

Keep the product definition narrow enough to underwrite.

Eligibility and exclusions are a diligence control. They help investors understand that BuySooner is designed for a defined capital-gap segment rather than broad rescue finance or whole-property exposure.

Appendix takeaway: eligibility discipline protects the product boundary and supports repeatable underwriting.
