Appendix 1 / Customer eligibility and exclusions

BuySooner is for mortgage-capable buyers with a defined upfront capital gap.

The product boundary is deliberately narrow: eligible customers must be able to support the senior mortgage, contribute equity from day one and fit the property, contribution and exit policy settings.

01

Mortgage-capable, not weak-credit

The customer must satisfy senior lender assessment and demonstrate capacity to service the first mortgage. BuySooner is not designed to rescue borrowers who cannot support senior debt.

02

Deposit-constrained, not deposit-free

The customer contributes equity into the transaction from day one. BuySooner supports the missing settlement contribution; it does not remove the need for customer capital at risk.

03

Standard residential property

The property should be ordinary, marketable residential property in an acceptable location, with sufficient resale depth and valuation reliability to support refinance or sale exit.

04

Exit viable before approval

The transaction must show a credible refinance or sale pathway within the policy period. If exit is not viable, the transaction should not proceed.

Eligibility discipline

The product works because the customer segment is specific.

BuySooner targets a capital-timing gap, not general affordability stress. Eligibility rules protect the model from drifting into weak-credit lending, speculative property exposure or unsupported exits.

Customer

Borrowing capacity established by senior lender

Equity

Customer capital committed from day one

Contribution

BuySooner contribution inside approved bands

Exit

Refinance or sale pathway tested upfront

Excluded from core pathway

What BuySooner should not fund.

  • Weak-credit or distressed borrowers who cannot support the senior mortgage.
  • Speculative, non-standard or illiquid property scenarios.
  • Transactions outside approved contribution bands or policy limits.
  • Cases where refinance, sale or controlled exit cannot be credibly demonstrated.
Appendix takeaway: eligibility discipline protects the product boundary. BuySooner is for mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained buyers with customer equity, standard property collateral and a credible exit pathway.
