Appendix 1 / Customer eligibility and exclusions

BuySooner is for mortgage-capable buyers with a defined capital gap.

Eligibility is deliberately narrow: the customer must support the senior mortgage, contribute equity and show a credible refinance or sale pathway.

01

Mortgage-capable

The customer must pass senior lender assessment and demonstrate capacity to service the first mortgage.

02

Deposit-constrained

BuySooner supports the missing settlement contribution. It does not remove the need for customer capital at risk.

03

Standard residential property

The property should be ordinary, marketable and capable of supporting refinance or sale exit.

04

Exit viable before approval

If a credible refinance or sale pathway cannot be shown, the transaction should not proceed.

Eligibility discipline

The product works because the customer segment is specific.

BuySooner targets a capital-timing gap, not weak-credit lending or general affordability stress.

What BuySooner should not fund.

Weak-credit borrowers, speculative or illiquid property, transactions outside contribution limits, or cases where refinance, sale or controlled exit cannot be credibly demonstrated.

Appendix takeaway: BuySooner is for mortgage-capable, deposit-constrained buyers with customer equity, standard property collateral and a credible exit pathway.
