Appendix 4 / Exit risk and fallback pathways

Explain what happens if refinance is delayed or exit is harder than expected.

This appendix expands the refinance-first, sale-fallback and recovery logic behind the contribution-layer model.

01

Refinance-first pathway

The preferred exit is customer refinance once senior debt, income capacity, valuation and contribution repayment can be supported.

02

Scheduled review

Year 3 review and ongoing monitoring help identify full exit, partial exit, sale or escalation pathways before maturity pressure emerges.

03

Fallback controls

Where refinance is not viable, sale, partial exit or recovery controls are used to avoid indefinite exposure and protect capital.

Investor concern

Exit risk is managed by process and timing controls.

The key point is that BuySooner is not designed as an indefinite exposure. It has a policy period, monitoring triggers and defined fallback pathways.

Appendix takeaway: downside management depends on early review, exit classification, refinance-first discipline and sale fallback.
