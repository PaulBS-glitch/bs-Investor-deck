Appendix 2 / Formula and worked example

The exit formula is simple and modelled upfront.

BuySooner has no monthly repayment, no interest and no compounding. Value is realised at refinance or sale under a pre-agreed formula.

Core formula

Initial contribution plus formula participation.

The exit amount is tied to the initial contribution, the property value at exit and the time outstanding.

Exit = ICC + (PV × r × T)
r = (1 / k) × (ICC / HV)
ICCInitial BuySooner contribution.
PVProperty value at exit.
rAnnual participation rate.
TTime outstanding.
HVHome value at settlement.
kPricing multiplier. Launch setting: 5.
Worked example

$1 million property, 10% BuySooner contribution.

The example shows the transaction mechanics under three annual property-growth assumptions.

Three-year exit sensitivity

Exit value is modelled using annual property growth. Participation = exit value × 2% × 3.
