Appendix 2 / Formula and worked example

The exit amount is formula-led, simple and sensitivity-testable.

BuySooner does not charge monthly interest, monthly repayments or compounding. The commercial outcome is realised at refinance or sale under a pre-agreed formula.

Core formula

Initial contribution plus formula-based participation.

The formula links BuySooner’s exit amount to the size of the initial contribution, the property value at exit and the period outstanding.

Exit = ICC + (PV × r × T)
r = (1 / k) × (ICC / HV)
ICCInitial BuySooner capital contribution.
PVProperty value at refinance or sale.
rAnnual participation rate.
TTime outstanding, measured linearly.
HVHome value at settlement.
kPricing multiplier. Launch setting: 5.
01

Purchase structure

$1,000,000 home value, $800,000 senior mortgage, $100,000 customer equity and $100,000 BuySooner contribution.

02

Participation rate

Contribution ratio is 10%. With k = 5, annual participation rate is 2%.

03

Illustrative exit

If property value at exit is $1,250,000 after 3 years, the participation amount is $1,250,000 × 2% × 3.

04

BuySooner repayment

BuySooner exit amount equals $100,000 initial contribution plus $75,000 formula participation.
